NORTH FITS BUSINESS MANAGER

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.

DATA SAFETY:
- Records are stored in this browser's local storage on this device.
- Before replacing/updating the app, open Backup & Settings and download a Full Backup JSON.
- If the updated app opens with empty records, use Backup & Settings > Restore Backup and select that JSON file.
- Keep the backup private; it contains customer contact details.

NEW IN THIS VERSION:
- Product Purchases ledger, separate from regular expenses.
- Optional order number and supplier on each purchase.
- Dashboard Overall Position (green plus / red minus).
- Cash position is calculated as cash received minus regular expenses minus product purchases.
- Purchases are not double-counted in net profit; enter the matching unit cost on the order so delivered product costs are used in profit.

ACCOUNTING NOTE:
This is a simple management tracker. Overall Position reflects recorded cash movement, not bank balance, inventory valuation, unpaid customer balances, or a formal accounting statement.
